A note on the numbers

Snapshot, not census. The file contains 30,000 records pulled from the Trumbull County voter registration system on March 30, 2026. It represents voters active on that date.

“Party” in Ohio is not registration. Ohio voters do not declare a party when they register. A party label is recorded only after a voter pulls a partisan primary ballot, and lasts for a limited window. The 70% labeled “Unaffiliated” here includes both lifelong independents and partisans who simply haven’t voted in a recent primary.

No voter history in this file. The voting history column was empty in the source data, so this snapshot can’t speak to turnout, only to who is registered.

No personal data is published here. Every figure on this page is an aggregate. Names, addresses, and dates of birth from the source file have not been republished and are not searchable from this dashboard. To check your own registration, use the Ohio Secretary of State at voterlookup.ohiosos.gov.
