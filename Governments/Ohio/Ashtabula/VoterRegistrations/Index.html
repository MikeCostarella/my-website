Source. Ashtabula County, Ohio voter file, dated 7 April 2026, obtained as a public record.

Method. All figures on this page were computed offline before publication. The page contains no individual records — only the aggregate counts you see in the charts. "Active voters" excludes registrants flagged as confirmation, NCOA, or list-maintenance status. Age bands are derived from birth year as of 2026; placeholder birth years (e.g., 1800) were excluded.

Caveats. Ohio's "party affiliation" reflects the most recent partisan primary a voter participated in, not a registration-time declaration as in some other states. A voter shown as "Unaffiliated" may hold strong partisan preferences but skip primaries. Turnout counts reflect the rolls as they exist today and do not capture voters who have since been removed.
