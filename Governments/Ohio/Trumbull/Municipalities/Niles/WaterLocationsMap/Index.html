The Quarter at a Glance

Across fifty-six funds, the City of Girard moved $1.84 million through its books in April alone, on pace with a four-month cumulative outlay of $6.71 million against a $24.80 million annual budget. The General Fund anchors the balance sheet at $2.31 million unexpended, while the Water Revenue Fund commands the largest share of activity — $1.59 million in YTD expenses with $2.43 million of work encumbered for the year ahead.
